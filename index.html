The Detail Process

How every car gets detailed

Four stages, in order, on every full detail — no stage skipped, no stage rushed.

01

Assess

We walk the car with you — paint condition, swirls, stains, problem areas — and agree the right level of clean before any work starts.

02

Wash & Decontaminate

Two-bucket hand wash and hand dry, wheels and tyres detailed, and the paint decontaminated so polishing works on a truly clean surface.

03

Correct & Deep Clean

Machine polishing lifts swirls and haze back to gloss while the interior gets steam cleaning, stain extraction and leather care.

04

Protect & Finish

Spray wax or ceramic coating locks in the gloss, trim is dressed, glass is finished — then every panel is checked against the M&M standard.

After the Detail

Keeping the shine

A detail is an investment. Three habits keep the gloss, the coating and the interior working for you between visits.

Hand wash, always

Automated brushes put the swirls back. A gentle two-bucket hand wash keeps polished paint and ceramic coating looking the way they left us.

Let protection do its job

Wax and ceramic coating are there to take the hit from sun, road film and water spots — so the clear coat underneath does not have to.

Stay on top of the interior

Regular vacuuming and wiping down leather and trim stops dirt grinding in — and makes your next interior deep clean go further.
